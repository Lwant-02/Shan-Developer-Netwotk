## Terms

Per the project owner, these fonts were built for Shan speakers and are free to use.
Recorded here so the attribution travels with the files.

For completeness, what is *not* established in writing: neither site states per-font
license terms, and `ajfonts.netlify.app` lists both free and commercial fonts without
saying which category these fall into. The binaries themselves embed "All Rights
Reserved" (`aj00` via an unfilled FontCreator template, `aj12` explicitly).

If you are redistributing this repository or reusing these fonts elsewhere, confirm
terms with the designer directly — contact details are on the sites above. Asking
about an OFL release, and about a bold weight, would settle both this and the
no-bold constraint in `design.md`.

Please keep this attribution intact.
